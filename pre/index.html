The opportunity

The problem is not merit.

Bihar-origin businesses in manufacturing, agriculture and services are investable. What is absent is the infrastructure between capital and business. So capital stays away, including the diaspora’s own, and a state growing faster than the country prices as if it were not.

The model

Structure is the product.

Metros have the layer that sits between capital and business: funds, auditors, boards, advisors. Bihar has the businesses and not the layer. We install it ourselves, as the entry condition rather than the aspiration.

01 · Assess

One framework, applied the same way

Financial health, governance readiness, market position, exit pathway clarity. The same evaluation for every company, because consistency is what makes a portfolio comparable.

02 · Build

The layer goes in first

Board rights established. Financial controls deployed. Monthly institutional reporting running. All of it before deployment, none of it promised after.

03 · Operate

We take the binding constraint

We sit on boards, install systems and take line responsibility for whichever function is capping growth, until it can stand without us.

The question every allocator asks

Where do the exits come from?

No exit market exists in Bihar today, so exits are not found. They are manufactured, company by company. ₹100 crore of revenue is the threshold at which mid-market capital enters, and building companies to that line is the explicit job of the operating layer rather than a hoped-for consequence of it.

Origination

Our pipeline is not sourced. It is built.

A proprietary programme
of Tier Two Capital

The 1 Cr. Circle is ours. We designed it, we run it, and it does not exist anywhere else. Eight companies, each past ₹1 crore in annual revenue, each twelve months into an engagement with us. Sixty days on Base Camp: an MIS the founder runs the week on, a two-year plan that survives investor questions, quarterly OKRs with named owners, a ranked map of what is capping growth. Then ten months on The Climb, where our team takes responsibility for the binding constraint.

Selection into the Circle is not an investment commitment, and no company in it is owed one. The work is to make a company investable. Whether the capital that follows is ours, another fund’s, or a bank’s is a separate decision, taken later and on its own merits.

What the Circle gives us is a year of operating evidence before any of those decisions is taken. That is diligence no data room reproduces, and it is why we can hold ten names rather than spread across twenty.
